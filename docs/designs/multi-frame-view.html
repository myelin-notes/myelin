Spatial Cognition in Digital Environments

The intersection of neurobiology and information architecture suggests that users navigate digital nodes similarly to physical landscapes. When spatial cues are present, retention rates for complex informational hierarchies increase by up to 34% (Miller et al., 2023).

"The mind maps concepts to coordinates before it maps them to semantic labels."

This leads to the hypothesis that the 'Infinite Canvas' UI pattern serves as an externalized hippocampus, allowing for the anchoring of abstract thoughts to fixed spatial coordinates. By doing so, we bypass the cognitive load of traditional window-switching...
